Digital twin maturity

A digital twin is a ladder,
not a label.

Four levels as defined by AWS. Our placement is a self-assessment.

LevelMeaningAblny today
L1 DescriptiveDigital description of the assetDelivered Barrier and bioprocess models
L2 InformativeConnected to sensor and historian dataDelivered Standard data ingestion, on public data
L3 PredictiveForecasts failure, yield or footprintDelivered Reliability and anomaly models with confidence intervals
L4 LivingLive data updates model parametersPlanned Needs a live operator or farm feed: the pilot goal

Source: AWS, Digital Twins on AWS leveling index (aws.amazon.com/blogs/iot).
