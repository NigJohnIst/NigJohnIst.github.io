AssetX Energy
NCS Well Integrity & Decommissioning Intelligence

AI-driven well integrity assessment, predictive asset health monitoring, and decommissioning intelligence for the Norwegian Continental Shelf — branded AblnyWellX in pilot and cluster engagements. Weibull-based remaining-useful-life and probability-of-failure scoring flags casing-integrity and flooding risk ahead of failure, informing P&A timing and reuse decisions before costly asset retirement. Originally benchmarked on the public Volve field dataset; validated to TRL 5/6 through demonstrations run on real operator data, reviewed by NCS technical boards, and now under discussion with leading NCS operators. Findings accepted for presentation at WCCUS 2026.

Open to validation
and scale-up projects.

Ablny is developing and validating AssetX™ with industrial, research and public-sector partners. We are open to additional European Commission and Norwegian projects where our working capabilities can be tested against defined operational problems, independent evidence requirements and measurable impact pathways.

Capabilities available

Asset integrity and P&A decision support; physics-informed and statistical modelling; digital-twin workflows; secure industrial AI; bioprocess and residue intelligence; monitoring, reporting and verification design; pilot data engineering; and auditable human-in-the-loop decision support.

Validation principle

We do not ask evaluators to accept claims on presentation alone. Each project defines a baseline, data-quality plan, model and uncertainty assessment, acceptance criteria, exploitation route and independent review where appropriate.

Projects we welcome

Research and Innovation Actions, Innovation Actions, demonstration pilots, cascade-funded projects, operator-led trials, living labs and cross-border consortia in energy transition, industrial AI, offshore integrity, methane and CO₂ monitoring, circular bioeconomy and resource efficiency.

What partners bring
